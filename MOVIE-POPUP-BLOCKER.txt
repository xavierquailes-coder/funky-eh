Movies Pop-up Blocker

- Added to berri://settings under Movies.
- Enabled automatically by default.
- Movie iframes use a sandbox without pop-up or top-navigation permissions.
- Parent window.open calls are blocked while the Berri movie player is open.
- Users can turn the blocker off in Settings if a player requires it.

Note:
Browser and iframe security rules can limit what a parent page can detect.
The sandbox is the main protection against pop-ups from embedded players.
